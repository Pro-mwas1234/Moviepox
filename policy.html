Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At Moviepox, your privacy is our premiere priority. This cinematic experience is built on trust, which is why we’ve designed our platform to be as transparent as a high-definition lens.

Moviepox is a no-trace platform. We do not collect, store, or profile your personal identities. Whether you're browsing the latest blockbusters or tracking your history, that data stays within your encrypted local environment or your private Firebase cloud session.

Our platform acts as a high-end interface for data provided by The Movie Database (TMDB) and external streaming mirrors. Note that these third-party agents may utilize their own tracking technologies according to their respective policies.

Moviepox has no control over these external entities. We act only as the director of your viewing experience.

As you navigate our cinematic universe, you may encounter links to official trailers or studio sites. Moviepox is not responsible for the scripts or security of those external sets.
